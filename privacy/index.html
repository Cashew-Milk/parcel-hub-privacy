Privacy Policy

Parcel Hub is an internal workflow extension for authorized shipping teams. It processes shipment data only to support ticket parsing, email parsing, carrier handoff, label tracking, and writeback workflows.

Data Processed

The extension may process shipment-related information from supported ticket pages, supported Outlook email pages, configured carrier pages, and internal host services. This can include names, company names, addresses, contact details, ticket identifiers, package details, customs descriptions, carrier selections, tracking numbers, and workflow diagnostics.

Storage

Parcel Hub may store local settings, queue state, parsed shipment state, tracking state, writeback state, and diagnostics in Chrome extension storage or through a configured internal native host. Storage is used to continue the shipping workflow and recover active work.

Sharing

Parcel Hub sends shipment data only to configured systems needed for the workflow, such as authorized ERP or ticket systems, UPS CampusShip, FedEx, WorldShip, and internal Parcel Hub host services. Parcel Hub does not sell data, use data for advertising, or transfer data for unrelated purposes.

User Control

Users can stop extension processing by disabling or removing the extension in Chrome. Local extension storage can be cleared through Chrome extension settings. Organization-specific data access, retention, and support requests should be directed to the user's Parcel Hub administrator.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact the Parcel Hub administrator for your organization or use the support information linked from this site.
